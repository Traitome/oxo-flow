# Human-Factors Evidence Status

> **Status**: simulation only.
>
> **Human participants**: none.
>
> **Important**: no SUS, NASA-TLX, time-to-first-pass, or preference result exists yet.

## 1. What the Simulator Can Establish

The synthetic response generator can exercise:

- reverse-scored SUS item handling;
- summary statistics;
- score-band mapping;
- the report's non-inferential evidence boundary.

It does not ingest real responses, run hypothesis tests, estimate effects, or
establish usability, cognitive load, learning time, accessibility, or user
preference.

## 2. Required Study Protocol

Before recruiting participants, the study must define:

- ethics approval and consent;
- target population and inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- task domains and equivalent difficulty across Nextflow, Snakemake, and `oxo-flow`;
- randomized or counterbalanced order;
- training and time limits;
- primary and secondary outcomes;
- handling of failed tasks, abandonment, and partial completion;
- SUS and NASA-TLX administration;
- accessibility accommodations;
- missing-data and outlier rules;
- analysis model and sample-size justification.

## 3. Required Reporting

The final report must include:

- recruitment flow and exclusions;
- participant experience distribution;
- every task outcome and timing;
- confidence intervals and effect sizes;
- mixed-effects or other pre-registered analysis appropriate to repeated measures;
- qualitative observations without converting anecdotes into frequencies;
- negative, null, and contradictory findings;
- raw response data or a controlled-access justification.

## 4. Current Decision

Human factors remain **Simulation only**. No usability claim may appear in a roadmap, release note, slide, or manuscript until real study data pass review.
